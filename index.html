Concept projects

These clients are made up. I built them to show how I think, from the problem to the direction. Nothing here is a client result.

Concept project

Schematiq

The problem
A data-analytics firm that looked too clinical. It struggled to connect with the people who actually make buying decisions in a crowded SaaS market.
The insight
Buyers trust numbers, but they remember people. The brand had to feel human without losing its precision.
The direction
A "dynamic flow" visual system. Technical precision on one side, soft organic shapes on the other, held together by one line: "Innovation that flows."
What I would measure
Event sign-ups, engagement on launch content, and how people describe the brand back to us.
Concept project

Sprig & Sky

A premium wellness brand with a generic look that did not justify its price. The direction was "Elevated Serenity": minimal layouts and luxury typography that make the brand feel like a high-end lifestyle. I would measure time on site and how many visitors book coaching or buy a plan.

Concept project

Iconography Co.

A creative agency with a traditional, static identity that hid its digital-first video work. The direction was a "Motion-First" identity with high-contrast visuals and editing-inspired layouts. I would measure enquiries from video clients and how often the showreel gets played.

The two-week brand fix

A small, clear first project. You see how I work before you commit to anything bigger.

  1. Days 1 to 3I read your product, docs and community. I find the one thing that confuses people most.
  2. Week 1I write your message and voice so it sounds like one team talking.
  3. Week 2I turn it into content and visuals people can use straight away. Your team reviews everything before it goes out.

What you get

  • A one-page message and voice guide
  • Three to five beginner-friendly threads or explainers
  • Two short video scripts
  • Visual direction notes for your graphics

We agree scope and budget on a 20-minute call first. I do not promise price pumps or overnight growth. I promise clear work and honest feedback.
